Multimodal Stress & Emotion Detection

Submit personal text reflections, spoken audio recordings, or both for weighted AI fusion analysis.

Multimodal Fusion (60:40)

Text analysis (RoBERTa) is weighted at 60%, while acoustic vocal features (Wav2Vec2) contribute 40%. Blending both prevents acoustic noise or flat speech from skewing results.

Fusion Score (0 - 100)

A composite index where 0 represents optimal calm/low stress, and 100 indicates acute emotional distress or burnout risk.

Risk Tier Classification
  • Low Risk 0 - 39: Stable emotional regulation.
  • Moderate 40 - 69: Elevated workload or stress tension.
  • High Risk 70 - 100: Persistent distress requiring relief.
Confidence vs. Intensity

Confidence is the AI model's mathematical certainty (0.0 to 1.0). Relative Intensity categorizes the expression into High Negative, Moderate, Neutral, or Positive.

Evaluated via RoBERTa deep learning emotion classifier with lexical fallback.
Supported: WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WebM (Max 16 MB).
